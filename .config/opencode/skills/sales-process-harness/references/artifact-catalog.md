# Stage and Artifact Catalog

Select only requested or decision-useful artifacts. In the workspace index,
mark each relevant item `Created`, `Pending`, or `Not applicable`; explain
pending inputs and omit unrelated items rather than generating a full checklist
of empty files. A request for multiple artifacts in the active stage should be
fulfilled even when the score is low or the recommendation is no-bid.

- **Intake / stalled opportunity:** Brief/current state; timeline and stall
  diagnosis; initial scorecard; stakeholder/questions; account or market research
  if requested and available.
- **Discovery:** Stakeholder/decision map; discovery questions; evidence and
  findings; updated scorecard; decisions, assumptions, and next actions.
- **RFI/RFP:** Requirement/compliance response matrix; evidence and source
  references; clarification questions; gaps, assumptions, and review risks;
  requested response sections.
- **Proposal:** Solution outline and options; delivery risks/dependencies;
  estimate structure; proposal outline; presentation storyline/speaker notes;
  meeting and follow-up drafts.
- **Decision:** Decision/options record; current scorecard and uncertainty;
  open commercial/scope decisions; next actions and draft follow-up.
- **Won / lost / no decision:** Outcome record; customer-stated reasons;
  separately labeled hypotheses; next steps; optional sanitized learning
  candidate.
- **Won-deal handover:** Accepted scope and approved commitments; solution
  assumptions, risks, dependencies, decisions, and open questions; delivery
  handover and owners to confirm.

## Artifact notes

- **Brief/current state:** identify the requested outcome, problem, current
  state, opportunity stage, evidence, known constraints, and missing inputs.
- **Timeline/stall diagnosis:** order dated events and source each date. Separate
  observed events from possible causes; suggest tests/actions rather than
  presenting a hypothesis as the reason for a stall.
- **Account/market research:** include only material public facts that can be
  sourced with URLs and access dates using active-runtime research capability.
  Do not infer private buying intent from public material.
- **Stakeholders/questions:** use role labels when names are not needed. Focus
  on decision process, outcomes, integration context, constraints, and evidence
  gaps; do not profile personal traits.
- **Solution/risk/assumptions:** expose options and dependencies. Mark technical
  feasibility as provisional until validated by the responsible specialists.
- **Estimate structure:** provide work packages, drivers, assumptions,
  dependencies, and fields for approved rates/quantities. Leave unsupported
  values blank or pending; do not invent pricing, margin, or effort policy.
- **Proposal/RFP:** distinguish supported response, conditional response,
  clarification needed, and unsupported/no evidence. Do not claim a capability
  solely because the customer requests it.
- **Meeting/follow-up drafts:** mark draft—not sent. The user reviews, edits,
  and sends outside this harness if they choose.
- **Presentation:** give storyline and speaker notes in Markdown. Export a PPTX
  only when specifically requested and a capable tool is present.
- **Outcome/learning:** quote or faithfully summarize customer-stated loss
  reasons separately from hypotheses. Present lessons as candidates for review
  and sanitization; never silently change shared skill rules.
- **Handover:** transfer only confirmed scope/commitments and clearly label
  open questions, assumptions, risks, and owners that still need confirmation.
